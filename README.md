A make.fun entry for "Robot Romance: One-Line Pickup Lines for AI". The program writes one single-line joke pickup line, aimed at a robot or an AI, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Do you believe in love at first prompt, or should I rephrase that?

"Love at first prompt" twists "love at first sight" with an AI-native word — no batteries, no charging, no reboot — and the punch lands in the final words.
